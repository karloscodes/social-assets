 Why take the job here, and not in a direct message 
 
 
 Every finished job builds your profile 
 A verified review, one more finished job on your count, a badge to post, and your name on the page of the brand as its hoodiechamp. A deal in a direct message leaves you nothing to show the next brand. 
 
 
 Finished jobs put you first 
 Creators with more confirmed jobs here show first to a brand. Each job you finish makes the next one easier to win. 
 
 
 You hold the final files until you are paid 
 Deliver a preview first, with a watermark. Send the final files when the money arrives. The brand gets its review and its free hire again only after it says it paid. 
 
 
 A record of every job 
 The price, the date, the use of the videos, your delivery and the confirmation are kept with their time. Print it if you ever need proof. 
 
 
 A way out 
 You or the brand can cancel a pick, with a reason, until you deliver. 
 
 
 Bring your own brands 
 A brand that already knows you can ask for an invite, then send a brief that only you see. The second time, it costs the brand nothing. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 You see only what fits. 
 Briefs show up when they match your kinds of work, categories, and languages. 
 
 
 Every finished job adds a review. 
 The brand you worked for writes it. It stays on your profile. 
 
 
 You keep 100% of your price. 
 We take no cut. The brand pays you directly, and joining costs you nothing.
