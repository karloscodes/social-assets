 Questions small businesses ask 
 
 
 What does a beauty video from a creator cost? 
 The creator names the price, so it differs. On Hoodiechamp you see several prices for the same brief before you choose. We publish real price ranges when enough briefs are finished. 
 
 
 Can I use the videos as paid ads? 
 Yes, when the brief says so. Every brief states how the videos may be used, from organic posts only to paid ads with no time limit. The creator sees that before naming a price, and the deal repeats it in writing. 
 
 
 Do I have to send my product? 
 Almost always. You and the creator agree on shipping by email after you pick. Hoodiechamp does not move products, files, or money. 
 
 
 Can the creator make claims about my product? 
 Write the rule in the brief, for example &quot;no medical claims&quot;. Cosmetic claims have legal limits in most countries, and the brand answers for its ads.
