 Made for one person, or five 
 You sell something real: a serum, a coffee, a candle, a pet toy, an app. You need videos, and you do not have a week to find someone on Instagram. Write one sentence. Read the prices. Pick one. 
 
 
 
 Why pick here, and not in a direct message 
 
 
 A record of the whole job 
 The pick puts the price, the date, and the use of the videos in an email to both of you. Every step after it is kept with its time: the delivery, your confirmation, the review. Print it if you ever need proof. 
 
 
 See a preview before you pay 
 The creator delivers a preview first. You pay them, they send the final files, and you confirm the job. 
 
 
 A way out 
 Plans change. You or the creator can cancel a pick, with a reason, until the work is delivered. Your next pick is free. 
 
 
 Hire again for free 
 Your next job with a creator who already finished one for you here costs nothing. One click starts it. 
 
 
 Your hoodiechamps, in one list 
 Every creator you finished a job with, what you agreed, and how you rated them. Show them on your public page if you want. 
 
 
 Bring a creator you know 
 Already agreed with someone who has a profile here? Send a request that only they see, and pick them. You get the record, and they get the review. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Watch first. Pick second. 
 Every creator shows real videos, a follower count, and reviews from finished jobs.
